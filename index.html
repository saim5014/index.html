Privacy Policy

Car Maintenance Tracker

Effective Date: October 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Car Maintenance Tracker. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application. The app helps vehicle owners manage vehicle details, mileage, maintenance history, expenses, and upcoming service reminders. We aim to provide a useful, offline-first experience while respecting your privacy.

2. Information Stored in the App

You may enter and store the following information:

  • Vehicle make, model, year, and other vehicle details.
  • Vehicle mileage and odometer readings.
  • Oil changes, engine services, brake services, tyre changes, battery replacements, and other maintenance records.
  • Maintenance dates, service descriptions, and expenses.
  • Upcoming maintenance schedules and reminder preferences.

This information is used to provide the vehicle-management features of the application.

3. Local Data Storage

Car Maintenance Tracker uses an SQLite database to store vehicle information, maintenance records, mileage, and expenses locally on your device.

The core application is designed to work offline and does not require an online account. Provided the installed version does not include additional data-transmission functionality, records stored in the local database are not automatically uploaded to servers operated by the developer.

Uninstalling the application or clearing its data may permanently delete locally stored records. Please keep your own backups if you need to retain this information.

4. Notifications and Reminders

The application may schedule local notifications to remind you about upcoming vehicle maintenance. These may include oil changes, scheduled services, tyre replacements, and other maintenance activities.

Local notifications are scheduled on your device and do not inherently require your maintenance records to be sent to an external server. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

5. Data Sharing and Sale

The application is designed to keep vehicle and maintenance records on your device. The developer does not sell your personal information.

This statement applies to the app's actual implementation. If advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, cloud synchronization, or other third-party services are included, their data practices must also be disclosed in this policy.

6. App Permissions

Depending on the features enabled in your version of the app, notification permission may be requested to deliver maintenance reminders. Permissions are used to support the corresponding application features.

7. Data Security

The primary vehicle and maintenance records are stored in the local SQLite database on your device. You should protect your device with a screen lock and keep its operating system updated.

No method of electronic storage is completely secure. Anyone who gains access to your device or accessible application data may potentially access information stored there.

8. Children's Privacy

Car Maintenance Tracker is intended for people managing vehicle ownership and maintenance. It is not specifically directed toward children. The developer does not intentionally collect children's personal information through a developer-operated server.

9. Data Deletion

You can delete vehicle and maintenance records using the application's available deletion features. Clearing the application's data or uninstalling it may remove the locally stored information.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features or data practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
